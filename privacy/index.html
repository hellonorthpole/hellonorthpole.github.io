Your family's privacy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

We only ask for what Father Christmas needs to make the call special, we never sell it, and we delete your child's details after Christmas.

The short version. A parent or guardian books the call and tells us a little about their child. We use that only to make the call, then delete it by 8 January 2027. Calls aren't recorded unless you choose a keepsake recording. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

We only collect a child's details from their parent or guardian, who confirms this when booking. We don't ask children for personal information during the call, and Father Christmas is set up never to ask for an address, school or surname.

The call itself

Father Christmas's voice is created with AI voice technology, guided by the details you share. During the call, what is said is processed in real time so he can reply. Calls are not recorded or kept unless you choose a keepsake recording at booking; if you do, we keep it only to send it to you, then delete it with the rest of your booking.

Advertising cookies (Meta)

Only if you tap Accept on our cookie message, we use Meta's pixel to measure whether our Facebook and Instagram adverts lead to visits, sign-ups or bookings. Meta processes this under its own privacy policy. If you tap No thanks, it doesn't run. You can change your mind by clearing this site's data in your browser.

How long we keep it

  • Booking and child details: deleted automatically by 8 January 2027.
  • Payment records: kept for six years, because the law requires it for tax.
  • Waitlist: until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you.

Cookies

We only use optional cookies (Microsoft Clarity) if you tap Accept on our cookie message. If you tap No thanks, they don't run. You can change your mind by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Your rights

You can ask to see the details we hold, correct them, delete them, or object to how we use them, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Just email hello@teethingissues.com and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
