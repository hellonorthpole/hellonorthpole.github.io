Questions parents ask

How can my child talk to Santa on the phone?

The easiest way is to let Father Christmas ring you. You book online in about three minutes and share a few details: your child's name and how to say it, their age, one or two proud moments from this year, their wish list, one gentle nudge and anything he should avoid. Then you pick an evening between 1 and 24 December. At that time your phone rings, you put it on speaker so the whole family can hear, and your child can chat with Father Christmas for about five minutes. It works on any UK mobile or landline, and there's nothing to download.

Is this an AI Santa? (for grown-ups)

Yes, and we'd always rather tell you straight. Father Christmas's voice is created with carefully designed AI voice technology, and every call is guided by the details you share when booking, so he can listen and answer naturally instead of reading a script. It isn't an open-ended chatbot your child uses alone: you book it, it rings your phone at the time you chose, it lasts about five minutes and then he says a warm goodbye. You're in the room, with the speaker on. He's always kind and gentle, never scary, and he never promises a specific present, so Christmas morning stays safe. For the child, it's a conversation with Father Christmas. The facts are for the grown-ups.

What if they go quiet or feel shy?

That's completely normal, and Father Christmas is ready for it. He's patient, he speaks gently, and he's happy to do most of the talking, using the proud moments and little details you shared. A nod, a giggle or a whispered "hello" is plenty. Having the speaker on and the family close by helps shy children feel safe. There's no pressure to say anything at all.

What will Father Christmas talk about?

He talks about the things you tell us when you book. He can mention one or two proud moments from this year, ask about the wish list, and add one gentle nudge if you'd like one, such as being kind to a brother or sister or going to bed nicely on Christmas Eve. You can also tell us anything to avoid, and he'll steer clear of it. He never promises a specific present, so there's no awkward moment on Christmas morning, and he always ends with a warm goodbye.
