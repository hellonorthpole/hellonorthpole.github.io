Questions parents ask

What is Father Christmas's address in the UK?

Write to Father Christmas, Santa's Grotto, Reindeerland, XM4 5HQ. Put your full return address on the letter so the reply can find you, and add a stamp.

Does Royal Mail reply to letters to Father Christmas?

Royal Mail has a long tradition of replying to letters sent to Santa's Grotto, Reindeerland, XM4 5HQ. Check Royal Mail's website for this year's details and last posting date.

When is the last day to post a letter to Father Christmas?

Royal Mail sets the date each year, so check their website for this year's last posting date. Posting early in December gives the reply plenty of time to arrive.

Can my child get a reply that talks back?

Yes. Hello North Pole arranges a live call from Father Christmas to your phone at the time you choose. If you mention the letter in the proud moments box when booking, he can talk about it on the call.

Is it really Father Christmas? (for grown-ups)
