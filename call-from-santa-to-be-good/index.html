Questions parents ask

Can Father Christmas call to help my child be good?

Yes, gently. When you book, you choose one small "gentle nudge" for Father Christmas to mention, such as being kind to a brother or sister, trying new vegetables, tidying toys away or going to bed nicely on Christmas Eve. He brings it up warmly, as encouragement rather than a warning, usually after talking about the proud moments you shared. A nudge can feel different coming from Father Christmas, and it can become something the family smiles about together. Think of it as a kind boost from the North Pole, not a behaviour plan.

Will Father Christmas mention the naughty list?

No. Father Christmas never threatens the naughty list, never tells a child off and is never scary. Every call is about encouragement. If you'd like a nudge, he'll give it kindly, once, and then move on to the fun bits: their wishes, questions about the reindeer and a warm goodbye. You can also tell us anything to avoid when you book, for example a recent worry, a family change or a topic that might upset them, and he'll steer clear of it.

What kind of gentle nudge works best?

Small, specific and positive works best. "Being a kind big sister to the new baby" or "going to bed nicely on Christmas Eve" is easier for a child to hear than "stop being naughty". Pick one nudge rather than a list, so the call stays happy and the message stays clear. Phrase it as what you'd love to see more of, not what's gone wrong. Father Christmas will weave it in kindly, and he never promises a specific present in return, so there's no awkward deal to keep on Christmas morning.

Is there a Santa video message to be good?

Hello North Pole is a live phone call, not a video message. That means your child can talk back: answer his questions, tell him about their list and hear him reply in the moment. Put the phone on speaker by the tree so everyone can listen in, and film their reaction if you like. You still end up with a video to keep, and it's one of your own family. Is it really Father Christmas? (for grown-ups)

For the child, it's a real conversation with Father Christmas. For parents, here is exactly how it works. Father Christmas's voice is created with carefully designed AI voice technology, and every call is guided by the details the parent shares when booking: the name, age, proud moments, one gentle nudge, the Christmas list and anything to avoid. Because of that, no two calls are the same, and Father Christmas can answer questions naturally instead of reading a script. We tell parents all of this up front, so the magic is for the children and the facts are for the grown-ups. Father Christmas is always kind and gentle, never scary, never promises a specific present, and keeps every call to about five minutes.
