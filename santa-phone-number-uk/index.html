With Hello North Pole it works the other way round: Father Christmas rings your phone at the evening you choose, and your child can chat, ask questions and tell him about their Christmas list. How does a phone call from Father Christmas work?

Booking takes about three minutes. You fill in a short form with your child's name and how to say it, their age, one or two proud moments from this year, their wish list, one gentle nudge (such as being kind to a brother or going to bed nicely on Christmas Eve) and anything Father Christmas should avoid. Then you pick an evening between 1 and 24 December. At that time your phone rings, you put it on speaker, and Father Christmas chats for about five minutes. He's always kind and gentle, never scary, and he never promises a specific present, so Christmas morning stays safe.
