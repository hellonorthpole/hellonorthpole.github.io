What is the NORAD Santa tracker?

NORAD Tracks Santa is the original Santa tracker, run every Christmas Eve by NORAD, the North American Aerospace Defense Command. The tradition began in 1955, when a newspaper advert printed the wrong phone number and a child rang a military operations centre in Colorado looking for Santa. The colonel on duty played along, and the Christmas Eve tracking has run every year since. Today the NORAD Santa tracker is a free website at noradsanta.org, with official apps and official social media updates. On Christmas Eve the NORAD map shows the sleigh moving from country to country. Exact start times are published on the NORAD website, so check noradsanta.org in December for the 2026 details.

What is the Google Santa tracker?

Google Santa Tracker is a free Christmas website from Google at santatracker.google.com. Through December the site becomes Santa's Village, with mini-games, quizzes and short animated videos for the countdown to Christmas. On Christmas Eve the Google Santa tracker map switches on, showing the current stop, the next stop and how far away the sleigh is. Google Santa Tracker works in a web browser, so a phone, tablet or laptop is enough, with nothing to install. The official Google site lists the times for 2026, so look there rather than at older articles.
