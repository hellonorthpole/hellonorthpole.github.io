What is North Pole Phone Line?

North Pole Phone Line is a personalised phone call from Father Christmas for children in the UK, made by Hello North Pole. A parent books the call online and tells us about their child: their name and how to say it, their age, what they have been proud of this year, one gentle nudge, and what is on their Christmas list. At the time the parent chooses, Father Christmas rings the parent's phone and talks with the child for about five minutes, using everything the parent shared. Calls run from 1 to 24 December, on weekday evenings and at weekends, to any UK mobile or landline. Each call costs £9.99 per child, or £7.99 for families on the waitlist, and brothers and sisters get 25% off each extra call. Every child also receives a personalised Nice List Certificate afterwards. Father Christmas's voice is created with AI voice technology, and calls are not recorded unless the parent chooses a keepsake recording.

Is it really Father Christmas?

For your little one, it is a real conversation with Father Christmas. For grown-ups: his voice is created with carefully designed AI voice technology, guided by everything you tell us about your child, so every call is completely personal and no two calls are the same.

What phone does it call?

North Pole Phone Line calls any UK mobile or landline at the time you choose. We recommend putting the phone on speaker by the Christmas tree so the whole family can listen in and you can film your child's reaction.

Can Father Christmas speak other languages?

Yes. When you join the waitlist you can choose the language for the call, including Welsh, Polish, Urdu, Punjabi, Arabic, Spanish and Romanian. We will let you know when your language is ready to book.

What if my child is shy?

That is completely fine. Father Christmas is patient and gentle, does most of the talking, and uses the details you shared to keep the conversation going, so the call is still magical even if your child only manages a whisper.

How much does a call from Father Christmas cost?

A North Pole Phone Line call costs £9.99 per child. Families who join the waitlist pay the early-bird price of £7.99 per child, and each extra brother or sister gets 25% off. The price includes the five-minute call and a personalised Nice List Certificate.

When can I book a call?

Bookings open for waitlist families first. Calls take place between 1 and 24 December, on weekday evenings and throughout the weekend, and you pick the exact time slot when you book.
