How do I write a letter from Santa to my child?

A good letter from Santa is short, warm and specific. Start with North Pole news: the reindeer, the elves, the snow. Next, mention one proud moment from this year, in simple words. Then add one gentle nudge, framed as a favour rather than a warning, so the letter stays kind. Finish with a sign-off from Father Christmas and a P.S. with one small detail from home. Five or six short lines is plenty for a young reader, and leaves room for the magic.

Thick cream paper, red or gold ink and a sticker seal make the letter look like North Pole post. Timing matters too: a letter that appears on the morning of 1 December, or inside a Christmas Eve box, feels like a delivery rather than a craft project.

Does Royal Mail send a letter from Santa?

Yes. Royal Mail replies to letters posted to Santa/Father Christmas, Santa's Grotto, Reindeerland, XM4 5HQ, and the reply itself is free. For 2026, Royal Mail asks for letters to be posted by Wednesday 9 December so the reply has time to arrive before Christmas, and says a reply can't be guaranteed.
