The terms, in plain English

  • Your gifted call: one family call from Father Christmas, booked with code FOUNDINGFAMILY for an evening from 3 to 30 November. Brothers and sisters are free too.

  • Your code: anyone who uses it at checkout gets 10% off their call.
  • What you earn: 25% of what they paid, or 30% with 10k+ followers. An early-bird call (£7.99, until 30 November) costs them £7.19 with your code, so you earn £1.79, or £2.15 at 30%. From 1 December it's £9.99, so £8.99 with your code: you earn £2.24, or £2.69 at 30%.

  • We pay by bank transfer each month once you've earned £10. Anything under £10 carries over to the next month.
  • When you first reach £10, we'll contact you privately for your bank details. We never ask for them on this page.
  • No commission on free or £0 bookings, refunded bookings, or your own booking.
  • Whenever you post your code, add #ad. You earn money from it, so UK advertising rules say it needs the label.
  • Please share honestly: no spam, no paid ads using our name, and no promises we can't keep.
  • Codes work on calls booked at hellonorthpole.co.uk for Christmas 2026. We may end a code or the scheme with notice, and we'll always pay what you've already earned.
