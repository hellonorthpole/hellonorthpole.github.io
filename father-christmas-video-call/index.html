Can you do a Father Christmas video call?

Yes, in two main ways. The first kind of Father Christmas video call is a personalised video message: a film made in advance, with a name or photo added, that plays like a call. The second kind is a live video slot, where a performer dressed as Father Christmas joins a video chat at a booked time. A live video call can be wonderful, though prices and quality vary a lot, so read the details before you book. For a side-by-side look at video messages, read Santa video message or a live call.

How do Santa calling apps work?

A Santa calling app lives on a phone or tablet. Plenty of Santa calling apps play a pre-recorded call or video that looks like an incoming call, sometimes with a name added. Some calling apps are free with adverts, and some charge for extra calls inside the app. A Santa calling app can be a fun surprise, but a pre-recorded call can't answer a question or hear the Christmas list. Before downloading any Santa calling app, check the reviews, the in-app prices and what the app does with your family's details.

What's different about a real phone call from Father Christmas?

A real phone call from Father Christmas rings your own phone, like any other call. With Hello North Pole there is no app to download and no screen to set up. Any UK mobile or landline works, including a grandparent's home phone. The call lasts about five minutes and is a two-way conversation: Father Christmas asks questions, hears the answers and replies. Every call is built around the details you share when booking, such as proud moments, the Christmas list and one gentle nudge. The Hello North Pole call is voice only, so there is no screen and no camera, just his voice.
