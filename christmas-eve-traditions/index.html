12 Christmas Eve traditions for UK families

Christmas Eve is the most magical night of the year. These are the little traditions UK families love, from the classics to a few new ones worth starting this year.

  1. Open a Christmas Eve box. New pyjamas, a festive film, hot chocolate and a book, opened after tea.
  2. Leave out a mince pie for Father Christmas. And a carrot for Rudolph. A small glass of milk (or sherry) is optional.
  3. Sprinkle reindeer food on the doorstep. Oats work well. Skip the glitter, because it isn't safe for birds and wildlife.
  4. Hang the stockings. At the end of the bed or by the fireplace.
  5. Read 'Twas the Night Before Christmas. Even better in matching pyjamas.
  6. Track Father Christmas's sleigh. Watching his journey across the world makes bedtime easier.
  7. A call from Father Christmas. A personalised call from Father Christmas, using your child's name and what they've done well this year. Book it for Christmas Eve teatime.
  8. Write a last-minute note to the elves. Pop it by the tree.
  9. A Christmas Eve walk to see the lights. Wrap up warm and count the houses with lit-up reindeer.
  10. Bake something together. Gingerbread or biscuits for the visitors tonight.
  11. Lay the table the night before. Crackers, place names and all.
  12. Early to bed. Because he only comes when everyone is asleep.
