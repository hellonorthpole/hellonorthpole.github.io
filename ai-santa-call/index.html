Questions parents ask

Is an AI Santa call safe for children?

It depends on who runs it, so ask the questions on this page of any service. Ours is booked by a parent or guardian, rings the parent's phone at the time they chose, and is set up never to ask a child for an address, school or surname. Calls aren't recorded unless the parent chooses a keepsake recording, and every booking and child's details are deleted by 8 January 2027. Hello North Pole is registered with the ICO.

How does the Father Christmas voice work? (for grown-ups)

Father Christmas's voice is created with carefully designed AI voice technology. During the call, what's said is processed in real time so he can reply, and he's guided by the details the parent shares when booking: name, age, proud moments, Christmas list, a gentle nudge and anything to avoid. We tell parents all of this up front, so the magic is for the children and the facts are for the grown-ups.

Is the call recorded?

No, unless you choose a keepsake recording at booking. If you do, we keep it only to send it to you, then delete it with the rest of your booking.

What if my child says something unexpected?

He's set up to stay kind, gentle and full of Christmas, and to keep to the things you shared. Because the call comes to your phone with the speaker on, you're right there and hear every word.

Does Father Christmas promise presents?

No. He might say he's heard about the list, but he's set up never to promise a specific present, so Christmas morning stays yours.
