How does an AI Santa call work?

Father Christmas's voice is created with carefully designed AI voice technology. The call is a real-time conversation: what's said on the line is processed as it happens, so Father Christmas can listen and reply. Father Christmas doesn't guess about your family. Every Hello North Pole call is guided by what a parent shares when booking: a first name and how to say it, an age, one or two proud moments, the Christmas list, a gentle nudge and anything to avoid. That's why the call feels personal. Because you told Father Christmas first.

What should parents ask before booking an AI Santa call?

  • Who books it?A grown-up. We only take a child's details from their parent or guardian.
  • What does it ask?Ours is set up not to ask for an address, school or surname.
  • Is it recorded?Not unless you choose a keepsake recording when you book.
  • When is it deleted?Every booking and child's details, by 8 January 2027.
  • Can you steer it?Yes. Tell us anything to avoid, and he's set up to steer well clear.
  • Is he kind?He's set up to be gentle, not scary, and not to promise a present.

Ask these 6 questions of any AI Santa call, not just ours. First, who books the call: a parent or guardian, or anyone? Second, what personal details does Father Christmas ask for on the line? Third, is the call recorded, and fourth, when is everything deleted? Finally, can a parent steer the topics, and is the voice set up to be gentle? Clear answers in writing are a good sign.

Can parents hear every word of the call?

Yes. Father Christmas rings your phone, at the time you chose. Not a child's phone. Put the speaker on, gather the whole family round the tree, and you hear everything Father Christmas says and every answer that comes back. The call lasts about five minutes, any evening from 1 to 24 December. Then a very happy goodbye from the North Pole.
