Founding Families

Last updated: 14 October 2026

We're inviting 50 UK families to try a call from Father Christmas this December before anyone else, and to tell their followers honestly what they thought.

The short version. Your child gets a call from Father Christmas at no cost to you. In return, we ask for one honest post about it, marked #ad. You can change your mind at any time.

What you get

  • A personal five-minute phone call from Father Christmas for your child, on an evening you choose between 1 and 24 December. It usually costs £9.99.
  • A keepsake recording of the call, if you'd like one.
  • A Nice List Certificate by email after the call, made out to your child by name.

What we ask

  • One honest post about the call on TikTok or Instagram, within 7 days of the call. Say what you really think.
  • Mark the post #ad. UK advertising rules say a free gift in return for a post is an advert, so the label is needed.
  • Tag @hello_northpole so we can find it and say thank you.

Using your post

We'd love to share your post on Hello North Pole's own pages, always credited to you. We only do this with your yes, and we'll take it down whenever you ask.

Looking after your child

  • Your booking works exactly like everyone else's and follows our privacy policy. We delete your child's details after Christmas.
  • We never use your child's name, face or voice in our own posts or adverts. The only exception is if you share a post yourself and then say yes to us resharing it.
  • If you choose a keepsake recording, it's yours. We keep it only to send it to you, then delete it with the rest of your booking.

Changing your mind

You can step out at any point, for any reason, before or after the call. Just tell us. There's nothing to pay and no hard feelings.

How to say yes

  1. Reply "I agree" to our message.
  2. Book your call at hellonorthpole.co.uk/book and enter the code we sent you at checkout. It takes the price to £0.
