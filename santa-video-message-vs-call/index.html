When a video message is the right pick

  • You want something to replay. A video can be watched again and again.
  • Family live far away. You can send a video on to grandparents.
  • They're not chatting yet. For a baby, watching might be all they need.

When a live call is the right pick

  • They've got questions for him. Is Rudolph ready? Did he get the letter? He answers.
  • You want a moment the whole room shares. Speaker on, everyone listening at once.

Questions parents ask

What's the difference between a Santa video message and a live call?

A Santa video message is a short film your child watches. Most are filmed once and then personalised with details you add, such as a name or a photo, so it plays the same way every time, and you can replay and share it. A live call is a real two-way conversation: Father Christmas rings your phone at the time you chose, your child can talk back and ask questions, and he answers. With Hello North Pole the call lasts about five minutes, works on any UK mobile or landline, and is guided by the details you share when booking, so no two calls are the same.

Are free Santa video messages any good?

Many families enjoy them, and a free video can be a lovely way to start the Christmas season. They're usually a pre-made film with a few details added in, which makes them quick and easy. If you'd like your child to actually talk with Father Christmas, ask him questions and hear him answer, that's where a live call is different. You can hear Father Christmas's voice for free at the top of this page before you decide.

Can you video call Santa?

Some services offer video calls. Hello North Pole is a phone call, voice only, and that's on purpose. It works on any UK phone with no app or Wi-Fi to sort out, and it leaves the picture of Father Christmas to your child's imagination: his armchair, the fire, the reindeer outside. You put the phone on speaker, the family gathers round, and everyone hears him together.

Which is best for Christmas Eve?

Both can work, but a live call suits Christmas Eve beautifully because it happens at a set time, like a little event. Christmas Eve is full of small rituals, such as new pyjamas, a Christmas story or putting out a mince pie and a carrot, and a call at teatime slots right in.
